HOW IT WORKED BEFORE

Three registers
and a WhatsApp group.

The store kept a register of receipts. The floor kept its own of output. Accounts reconciled both at month-end, and a buyer asking about an order got whoever picked up the phone.

Everest Textile OS keeps the GRN, the job card, the roll label and the invoice in the same database. “Where is my order?” becomes a lookup instead of three calls.

FOUR QUESTIONS, EVERY SHIFT

Asked forty times
a day.

Each of these used to mean walking the floor or calling someone. Each one now has a screen.

01 · STORE

Can this order start?

The recipe says 0.18 kg of 30s per metre. The screen multiplies, subtracts what is already issued and what earlier orders are owed, and shows the gap in kilograms.

YARN AVAILABILITY
02 · FLOOR

What is running now?

Every loom’s status is one tap on the supervisor’s phone. Mark a breakdown, describe it in a sentence, and the fitter or electrician who should go is named.

LIVE MACHINE BOARD
03 · QUALITY

Where did this roll come from?

The label on every roll is a QR code. Scan it at the bench for the order, loom, shift, operator and yarn issued. Rework goes back in the queue with its history attached.

YARN LOT→LOOM 04→ROLL QR→QC
04 · OFFICE

What is ready to ship?

Open a truck and scan rolls onto it. A rejected roll, a roll from another order or one already loaded is refused at the scanner. Close the truck and the invoice is done.
