Yarn arrives. Orders move through machines and shifts. Rolls pass inspection, then leave for a buyer. When those steps live in separate notebooks and chats, small gaps turn into delays, stock surprises and unanswered questions.

Everest Textile OS connects the work in one place, so every team can see what needs attention next.
